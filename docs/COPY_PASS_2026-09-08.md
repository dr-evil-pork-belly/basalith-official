# Basalith site copy pass, September 8, 2026

Site-wide rewrite of public-facing copy in `basalith-official`. Goal: intelligent
but plain, human, and pointed at a next step on every page. Every change is
under the standing rules: no em dashes in rendered copy, American English,
banned words avoided, Legacy Guide never Archivist, the two-line tagline and
the category line untouched, and no number, guarantee, or mechanism that cannot
be pointed to as real.

Twenty-two files changed. Legal pages (`/terms`, `/privacy`) and the demo
persona copy (`docs/BASALITH_DEMO_PERSONA_COPY.md`, `/succession/demo`) were
deliberately not touched. All changed files parse cleanly. Nothing was
deployed; David reviews, runs `vercel` for preview, and promotes.

## What changed, and why

### The pattern across the site

The core spine already read well. What it lacked was pull. Almost every CTA was
"Begin" or "Start the conversation," and the pages explained the product more
than they made the reader feel the cost of waiting. So the rewrite did three
things everywhere:

1. Led with the reader's situation, not the product. "The operator knows when to
   walk from a deal, which hire to trust over the resume, how much risk is too
   much. None of it is in the data room."
2. Made CTAs specific and low-stakes. "Tell us about the transition." "Talk to us
   about an acquisition." "Request a Founding Session." "See the demo."
3. Put the refusal on the page. The one thing no competitor sells is what the
   system will not say. It now appears, honestly worded, on /succession,
   /method, /faq, /integrity, /continuity and /families.

Also: business CTAs now route to `/apply?type=succession` or
`/apply?type=acquisition` (the typed form that persists and notifies you)
instead of the generic `/contact` form.

## Things I kept but could not verify. Please confirm or cut.

These were already on the site. I left them because removing a listed feature
changes the offer, and only you know which are real.

1. **Succession tier features** (/succession and /pricing): "Quarterly
   calibration sessions," "Board-level reporting," "Priority support," "Annual
   accuracy report." `lib/entityAccuracy.ts` exists, so the report is
   plausible. The other three I cannot point to in the code.
2. **Founding deliverables** (/pricing): "Document Compatibility Review.
   Attorney-ready output," "Contributor Network Setup. Onboarding for up to 15
   contributors," "Data Migration," "Custodian Designation." If any of these is
   not a real step in the founding, it should come off.
3. **Contributor limits**: /pricing says up to 15, /posthumous-archive says up
   to 10. One of these is wrong or they are different products. Pick one.
4. **Witness Archive pricing**: $4,500 founding and $3,600/year. Not in
   BUILD_CONTEXT or the pivot doc. Kept as published.
5. **48-hour response promise**: on /contact, /faq and /apply. It reads well and
   it is a real commitment. Make sure you can keep it.
6. **"Extended 3-hour founding session"** for business (/succession, /pricing)
   vs. 90 minutes for individuals. I wrote the site to say both. Confirm the
   three hours is what you actually deliver.
7. **"We start with the hardest calls the operator ever made"** (/succession
   step 01, /founding-session). Taken from the pitch narrative and the
   incident-session code. Confirm it describes the succession-tier founding,
   not only the acquisition intensive.
